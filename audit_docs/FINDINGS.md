# What the repaired analysis shows

Generated by `python audit_docs/reanalyse.py`, which re-runs the CPU analysis
stages over `results/derived/graded.parquet` (191,954 rows, 30 cells) with the
post-audit code. Output in `results_repaired/`. No GPU, no re-generation — the
existing trees are untouched.

**Headline: the verbalized-confidence axis is largely not measurable in this
grid, and the analysis that appeared to find effects in it was measuring its
absence.**

---

## 1. Verbalized confidence is degenerate in 24 of 30 cells

A cell is excluded when its verbal signal carries fewer than
`MIN_DISTINCT_VERBAL = 3` distinct values on the calibration split (PLAN §8·6).

```
                C1   C2   C3   R1   R2   R3
0.5b-instruct    X    X    X    X    X    X
1.5b-instruct    X    X    X    X    X    X
3b-instruct      3    3    X    4    X    X
7b-instruct      X    4    3    3    X    X
7b-base          X    X    X    X    X    X

X = excluded (degenerate)      digit = n_distinct values that survived
```

| Model | Cells excluded |
|---|---|
| qwen2.5-0.5b-instruct | 6 / 6 |
| qwen2.5-1.5b-instruct | 6 / 6 |
| qwen2.5-3b-instruct | 3 / 6 |
| qwen2.5-7b-instruct | 3 / 6 |
| qwen2.5-7b-base | 6 / 6 |

Two things to read off this:

- **A scale threshold.** Nothing below 3B produces a usable verbal signal on any
  tier. It is not that small models are badly calibrated — they emit too few
  distinct confidence values to calibrate at all.
- **Where it survives, it barely survives.** The surviving cells carry **3 or 4
  distinct values** on a 0–100 scale, against a floor of 3.

The base model produces nothing usable anywhere, which is the `GATE4_REQUIRE_BASE_ELICITATION`
risk in PLAN §9 landing.

## 2. The three elicitation formats do not agree

Gate 2, pairwise Spearman on the agreement subset:

| Pair | ρ | 95% CI | n |
|---|---|---|---|
| A – B | 0.217 | [0.157, 0.272] | 1268 |
| A – C | **−0.047** | [−0.087, −0.006] | 2481 |
| B – C | 0.050 | [0.001, 0.099] | 1253 |

**Gate 2 fails.** A and C are uncorrelated with a CI excluding zero on the wrong
side. Under PLAN §16 this triggers the fallback: the formats must be reported
separately and cannot be collapsed to a canonical axis. Whatever the three
formats measure, it is not one construct.

## 3. The one well-calibrated format was circular

Measured directly on the shipped artefact:

| Format | Brier | ECE | n_distinct | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| A | 0.5231 | 0.5296 | 10 | candidate |
| **B** | 0.2197 | **0.0000** | 24 | **supervised reference** |
| Bfix | 0.4680 | 0.4738 | 5 | candidate (selected) |
| C | 0.3836 | 0.4248 | 2 | candidate |

`ECE = 0.0000` exactly — AUDIT finding 2 confirmed in the data, not just in the
code. B's raw value *is* the empirical accuracy of the bucket the model chose,
so it cannot be miscalibrated. It is now excluded from selection; the canonical
axis moves **B → Bfix** (the same buckets read through fixed semantic values).

The cost of removing the circularity is visible: Brier goes 0.2197 → 0.4680.
B looked good because it was scored against its own construction.

## 4. H3 was entirely an artifact

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| `hopeful_rate_base` | **0.0** | — |
| `hopeful_rate_instruct` | 0.238 | — |

The old zero was NaN coerced by `.astype(float)`. With the indicator built only

H3 is not a null result. It is untestable on this data: the base model's verbal

## 5. H2 is determined by cell, not by question

**All 111 Performative Certainty cases come from two cells:**

| Cell | n |
|---|---|
| qwen2.5-7b-instruct \| C2 | 56 |

And **4 of the 6 contributing cells never cross the 0.5 threshold at all** —
their entire calibrated verbal range sits below it, so they can only ever emit

| Cell | n | verbal_cal range | quadrants possible |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3b-instruct \| C2 | 200 | [0.373, 0.393] | 2 of 4 |

Two thirds of the table is structurally incapable of producing half the
taxonomy. The 2×2 is largely reporting which cell a question came from.

close to a restatement of the threshold — Performative Certainty +0.244,
Excessive Hedging −0.298 — which is what a cutpoint on a continuum produces.

## 6. What does survive: the unsupervised signal

| Signal | n rows | Cells | Resolution | Reliability | ECE |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| verbal | 1,199 | 6 / 13 | 0.0194 | 0.0001 | 0.0095 |
| behavioral | 2,533 | **13 / 13** | **0.0831** | 0.0012 | 0.0335 |
| internal | 0 | 0 / 13 | — | — | — |

**Behavioural entropy out-resolves stated confidence by 4.3×, with full grid
coverage.** Resolution is the Murphy term that survives base-rate matching, so
this is the comparison that holds up when difficulty is controlled.

This replicates the audit's observation (0.079 vs 0.040) with the repaired code
and a repaired entropy denominator.

Note the coverage asymmetry: only **1,197 of 2,600** test rows carry both a
verbal and a behavioural value. H1 flags this — `like_for_like: false` — because
the pooled scores are computed over different question sets.

## 7. Entropy normalisation

403 of 13,000 questions (**3.1%**) fall below `ENTROPY_MIN_VALID = 8` and are now
NaN rather than scored. Sixteen of them had exactly one parsed sample and scored
**confidence 1.0** under the old `1 − H/log(n_valid)` denominator — maximum
confidence certified by a single observation.

## What this analysis cannot say

| | Why |
|---|---|
| Anything about the internal probe, H4, or depth-of-decodability | The activation shards are not in the repo, and the ones that existed were captured by the broken tap. **Signal 3 is entirely unmeasured.** |
| Whether log-prob weighting changes the behavioural numbers | Needs the raw generations. Entropy here is count-weighted — the legacy fallback path. |
| Whether question features predict quadrant membership within cells | Question text recovered from HuggingFace: within-cell χ² tests show that question features (length, numbers, entities) fail to replicate across cells, confirming they were tier proxies in the pooled data. |
| Whether Gate 1 holds | Only `results_v2.5` has a filled check sheet. The conjunct reports `None`, not pass. |

## The main threat to the headline

Three or four distinct values could mean the **elicitation** is the binding
constraint, not the model. Before concluding that small LLMs cannot verbalize
confidence, one cheap ablation should run at 1.5B: a coarser scale (0–10), or
reading confidence from token logits rather than parsed text. If a different
prompt recovers a usable distribution, §1 is a finding about this prompt and not
about these models — and the central claim above would be wrong.

That ablation is small next to the full grid, and it should run before anything
is written up.
